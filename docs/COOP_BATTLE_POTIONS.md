# Co-op battle potions: focused candidate

This source candidate adds Potion, Super Potion, Hyper Potion and Max Potion for the acting trainer's active Pokémon in shared wild and trainer battles. The ordinary Gen 3 HP amounts are preserved. The in-bag confirmation explicitly says the target is the active Pokémon. Full-HP or fainted targets cannot use these items. Status cures, revives, PP items, other medicines and selecting reserve Pokémon are not included.

Selection does not consume the item. At execution the authoritative engine validates the requested item and active HP, then sends a session-scoped command to the owning controller. Only that controller can validate its pending selection and debit its own bag. The engine waits for its reply and uses the standard battle HP-bar and HP-update script. It never reads or debits the partner's bag. Potion debits are tracked separately per trainer and refunded if an uncommitted shared battle aborts or its private party merge is rejected; a valid bilateral completion retains them. A failed refund leaves its ledger intact and prevents a new co-op battle until a retry succeeds.

Co-op exchange protocol version increases from 3 to 4. Both players must use the updated build; older peers are rejected at the initial exchange rather than failing halfway through a battle. This is a compatibility change that must be included in the combined candidate and release coordination. Solo and ordinary non-co-op battles retain their existing item behavior.

Focused tests compile the production ownership/action and potion code against disposable stubs, including both owner slots, malformed or stale commands, no-effect cases, repeat consumption, ordered acknowledgements and abort refunds. Existing co-op and battle payload regressions also run. These are not a native game build or live two-client battle, graphical bag confirmation, disconnect/WAN, real save/reload or proof that every item or party target is supported. Test the combined candidate on disposable profiles before release.
