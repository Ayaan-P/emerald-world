# Consequential character-to-character cooperation

This extends the existing cast's autonomous project execution with independent
character decisions. It is not a meeting/travel animation or a player quest.

An owner can add up to two existing actors to worksite.partners. Committing the
owner's retained construction intention publishes an exact invitation containing
its actor, task and versioned project signature. A followed recipient gets a
bounded ordinary author turn. It may join, decline, wait, or change its own goal.
No relationship, silence, or owner decision implies that another actor agreed.

The recipient's typed cooperate field contains owner, intention, signature and
decision (join or decline). Admission requires the exact inspected invitation
and current owner project. The actor must be named in that project. The
reaction-response guard permits only the current recipient's action, not edits
to another character's decision.

The authority executes only after every invited participant has independently
joined that exact current project. Declining or waiting has an actual consequence:
no geometry is changed. Changes to the owner version or project invalidate old
agreement, requiring fresh decisions. Removing the intention prevents work.
Original solo projects retain their existing behavior.

Before the atomic map commit the authority rechecks current participants. The
completed world event freezes the agreed actor list alongside real geometry
changes. Both owner and partners can receive a result-driven author turn.
Either may form a directional assessment of another participant from that
committed result; the other is not forced to reciprocate. An invitation alone
cannot justify a relationship assessment. Completed agreement cannot be rewritten
retroactively, though an unchanged past decision may remain in a later record.

Player-visible effects are the actual constructed courtyard/access path, world
journal invitations/decisions/results, original actors' evolving dialogue/goals,
and /guild cast ACTOR construction showing current project decisions. No player
participation, shared host narrative ownership, new NPC copy, story flag,
canonical party, private conversation or native script change is involved.

"Participation" here means an independent project decision recorded by the
authority and shared responsibility for its abstract execution. It is not proof
of physical labor, a journey, a meeting, resource gathering or affection. No
live model call or full rendered simulation has been verified.

Production-authority tests cover invitation-triggered recipient work, independent
decline preventing construction, later join enabling it, stale project agreement
refusal, actual shared result informing a one-way relationship, and persistence.
Maya owns integration, rendered QA and release.
